# Bound Remains — second draft

The accepted silhouettes, anatomy and native pixel detail carry forward exactly. The revisions concentrate on material colour and the evolved state, so each creature remains recognisable from its first draft. Front, back and side retain their common ground anchor.

Evolved cloth, leather, coffin wood, metal and brass receive a small reduction in brightness. The existing hue relationships and individual shade steps remain intact. Bone, masks, faces and spell flames keep their original colours to preserve the contrast that describes the anatomy.

A shared violet aura identifies the evolved state across all four families. Five faint bands extend outwards from the silhouette, fading to transparency. The aura sits behind the crisp pixel art; its strongest band remains understated. Precise spacing around the aura prevents the board from cutting off its outer edge.

The aura is exported separately from the darker body art as well as in the composite draft poses. A short, restrained opacity cycle supplies an animation reference for each direction. These are aura previews attached to static poses; the creature's movement, attacks and death remain a later authoring pass.
