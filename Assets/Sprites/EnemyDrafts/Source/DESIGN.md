# Bound Remains

Silhouettes carry the identity first. Narrow robes and a hood identify the acolyte; a forward skull and reaching limbs identify the pursuer; a broad funerary collar identifies the chorister; a coffin and heavy stone body identify the familiar. The evolved forms amplify those existing structures with mutations taken from the project's lore.

Cool violet shadows connect the figures to the existing Collector. Cloth uses long, interrupted folds; bone uses short bevels and recessed cavities; stone uses large planes and broken seams; the coffin has grain, a shaped lid and iron bindings. Detail comes from describing those materials at native resolution, without blur or random noise.

The supernatural accents are restrained. Amber belongs to the acolyte's casting hands, red to the pursuer's recessed face, cyan to the chorister's throat, and orange to the familiar's core. These accents remain secondary to anatomy and costume, so a monochrome outline can still distinguish the four families.

All directions use the Collector's 160 × 128 frame convention and a common ground anchor. Front, back and right profile are drawn independently. The review board enlarges them with nearest-neighbour sampling and includes the existing Collector as a pixel-density reference. These are static draft poses; animation is a later authoring pass.
